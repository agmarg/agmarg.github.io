**Portuguese Central Camino**  
10 days, 250 km — from Porto to Santiago de Compostela.  
Started on October 13 in Porto and arrived on the afternoon of October 22 in Santiago.

What a journey. The joy I experienced is beyond words.  
If St. James wills it, I’m already preparing — in spirit — for the Frances route.
